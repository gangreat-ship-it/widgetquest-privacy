WidgetQuest Privacy Policy · Effective date: 25 November 2026 WidgetQuest is a productivity and task-management application that helps users manage priorities, track deadlines, earn experience points (XP), collect virtual rewards, and view upcoming priorities through Android home-screen widgets. WidgetQuest does not require users to register, create an account, or sign in to use the application. 1. Information handled by the app Depending on the features you use, WidgetQuest may store information you enter or generate in the app, such as: Task and priority descriptions. Task deadlines and scheduled dates. Task completion status. Game progress, including levels, XP and virtual gold. Virtual inventory and companion pet selections. Information displayed in the WidgetQuest home-screen widget. This information is used to provide the app's task-management, game-progress, reminder and widget features. 2. Local storage WidgetQuest uses a local SQLite database to store app information on your device. The app does not require an online user account. Task information and game progress are intended to be stored locally for the app's functionality. 3. Notifications WidgetQuest may request permission to send local notifications about upcoming task deadlines. Notification scheduling is performed on your device. You can manage or disable notification permissions through your Android device settings. 4. Home-screen widgets WidgetQuest's Android home-screen widget can display upcoming priorities, including task titles, deadlines and countdowns. Widget content is supplied by the app from its locally stored data. Information shown on your home screen may be visible to anyone who can view your device. 5. Advertising and analytics WidgetQuest does not currently display advertisements. This statement does not, by itself, establish whether any third-party software component collects diagnostics or usage information. The developer will update this policy and the applicable store disclosures if the app's data practices change. 6. Sharing and third parties WidgetQuest does not provide an account-based service for sharing your tasks or game progress with other users. We do not sell users' personal information. The app's bundled libraries and services should be reviewed alongside this policy; any third-party data collection or transmission will be disclosed here and in the applicable Google Play Data safety disclosures. 7. Data retention and deletion App information is retained in local storage while it is needed by WidgetQuest. You can remove locally stored app information by clearing the app's data or uninstalling WidgetQuest. This may permanently delete tasks, progress, rewards and other locally stored information. 8. Children's privacy WidgetQuest does not ask users to provide personal information through account registration. The app's intended audience and any applicable age restrictions are declared separately through its Google Play target-audience and content-rating settings. 9. Security WidgetQuest uses storage mechanisms provided by the Android application environment for locally stored information. You should protect your device with appropriate screen-lock and security settings. No electronic storage method can guarantee absolute security. 10. Changes to this policy This Privacy Policy may be updated when app functionality, data practices or applicable requirements change. The latest version will be published at this webpage with its effective date. 11.
